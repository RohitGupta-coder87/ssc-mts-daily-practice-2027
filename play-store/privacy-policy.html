Privacy Policy

SSC MTS Daily Practice 2027
Effective date: 2 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how SSC MTS Daily Practice 2027 (“the App”) handles information. The App is an independent educational practice product created by Rohit Kumar Gupta and is not an official government or Staff Selection Commission application.

Information stored on your device

The App stores quiz scores, accuracy, practice history, bookmarks and streak information locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the App's learning and progress features. The developer does not receive this local learning history. You can remove it by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

Advertising and third-party processing

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived general location, ad interactions, app interactions and diagnostic information to provide, secure, measure and improve advertising services. The exact processing can depend on your device, region and consent choices.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at Google's partner-sites policy and review Google's Privacy Policy.

Accounts and personal details

The current App does not require an account and does not directly ask for your name, phone number, contact list, photos, precise location or payment details.

Children

The App is intended for adult competitive-exam learners aged 18 and above and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact us so it can be addressed.

Security

Network requests made by the App use encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but reasonable steps are used to reduce risk.

Changes

This policy may be updated when App features, advertising practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be revised when a material update is published.
