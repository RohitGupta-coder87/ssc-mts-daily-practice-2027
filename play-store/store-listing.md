## Full description

Prepare smarter for SSC MTS with focused daily practice.

SSC MTS Daily Practice 2027 gives aspirants an easy, distraction-free way to practise English, Mathematics, Reasoning and General Awareness. Attempt a fresh Daily Quiz, take a Full Mock Test or strengthen one subject at a time.

KEY FEATURES

• Daily Quiz with 25 questions  
• Full Mock Test with 40 questions  
• English, Mathematics, Reasoning and General Awareness practice  
• Clear Hindi/Hinglish explanations  
• Bookmark difficult questions for revision  
• Track scores, accuracy and practice history  
• Build a daily practice streak  
• Offline question bank  
• Clean interface designed for focused study

This first release contains 80 original foundation-level practice questions. More original practice sets and improvements will be added in future updates.
